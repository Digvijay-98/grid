The Lamborghini Urus is a high-performance luxury super SUV powered by a 4.0-liter twin-turbo V8 engine, with base ex-showroom prices in India starting at ₹4.18 crore. Available configurations include the versatile Urus S, the track-focused Urus Performante, and the plug-in hybrid Urus SE flagship delivering a combined 800 hp.

Features of CAR

Engine and Performance

Powertrain: 4.0L twin-turbo V8 engine, paired with an electric motor in the hybrid SE variant for up to 800–812 CV combined power.
Acceleration: Goes from 0 to 100 km/h in roughly 3.3 to 3.5 seconds depending on the spec.
Top Speed: Reaches up to 312 km/h.
Braking: Massive carbon-ceramic brake discs for supreme stopping power

Design and Exterior:

Aerodynamics: Sculpted hood, sharp body creases, rear diffuser, and a flat underbody with active vents.
Lighting: Distinctive Y-shaped LED matrix headlamps and tail lights.
Wheels: Lightweight alloy options up to 23 inches

Top Speed:

The Tamburo: The central control console used to manage engine response and gear selection.
ANIMA Selector: Drive mode selector offering modes like Strada (street), Sport, Corsa (track), Terra (dirt), Sabbia (sand), Neve (snow), and Rally.
Cabin Comfort: Premium Alcantara or leather upholstery, customizable contrast stitching, and power-adjustable massage seats

Handling and Dynamics:

All-Wheel Drive: Permanent four-wheel drive system with active rear torque vectoring and a Torsen central differential.
Steering & Suspension: Standard four-wheel steering for lower-speed agility and high-speed stability, paired with adaptive air suspension or stiffer track setups
ANIMA Selector: Driving mode controller featuring Strada, Sport, Corsa, Sabbia (sand), Terra (gravel), and Neve (snow) options.

Specification

  • Engine: 4.0-liter twin-turbocharged V8 + electric moto
  • Displacement: 3,996 cm³
  • Combined Power: 800 CV / 789 bhp @ 6,000 rpm
  • Torque: 950 Nm
  • Top Speed: 312 km/h
  • Transmission: 8-speed automatic with AWD
  • Seating :2+3
  • Fuel:ELECTRIC
  • Battery Capacity: 25.9 kWh lithium-ion
  • Electric-Only Range: Up to 60–66 km (WLTP)

Variants
