paneer tikka

is a famous North Indian starter. It features cottage cheese cubes and veggies marinated in thick yogurt and rich spices, then grilled until smoky, tender, and delightfully charred.

Paneer Tikka Ingredients

  • Paneer: 250 to 300 grams cut into large 1-inch cube
  • Vegetables: 1 large diced onion and 1 medium green or red capsicum (bell pepper), cut into square chunks
  • Curd/Yogurt: 1/2 cup thick hung curd or Greek yogurt
  • Oil: 1.5 to 2 tablespoons mustard oi
  • Binder: 1 to 2 tablespoons roasted besan (gram flour) to hold the marinade togethe
  • Aromatics: 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 to 2 teaspoons fresh lemon juice
  • Color & Heat: 1 to 1.5 teaspoons Kashmiri red chili powder
  • Earthiness: 1 teaspoon coriander powder and 1/2 teaspoon cumin powder
  • Warm Spice: 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • Tang & Finish: 1 teaspoon chaat masala and 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • Aroma: 1 teaspoon crushed kasuri methi
  • Optional: Pinch of turmeric powder or carom seeds
