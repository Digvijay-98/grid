Arctic Wolf (Canis lupus arctos)

A white-coated subspecies living in the extreme cold of northern Canada

Mexican Wolf (Canis lupus baileyi)

Also called the "Lobo," it is the rarest gray wolf subspecies in North America

Indian Wolf (Canis lupus pallipes)

A smaller, less vocal subspecies adapted to the warmer plains of Southwest Asia and India

Tundra/Siberian Wolf (Canis lupus albus)

A large, nomadic subspecies that roams the freezing Russian and Siberian tundras.

Timber Wolf (Canis lupus lycaon)

A forest-dwelling subspecies found in the Great Lakes region and parts of Canada
